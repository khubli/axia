# What a good role looks like for me

Claude reads this file together with your resume for every role. Be specific: the
clearer the hard stops and the soft gaps, the more the scores agree with you.
Roles are scored again after you change this file.

## Who I am
One or two sentences: your level, your domain and what you are known for.

## What I'm looking for
- The kinds of role (for example: engineering manager for a payments platform team)
- The level (for example: manager of managers, or a senior individual contributor)

## How to weigh things
- What makes you apply (for example: owning a platform, leading teams of 10+)
- Which missing skills are soft gaps that cost a few points, not a reason to skip
- Which roles look right on paper but are a step down for you

## Location and eligibility (hard constraints)
- Where you can work, and whether you need visa sponsorship
- Your target compensation, if you want it weighed

## Not in my background
Things a job may ask for that you don't have. A gap, or a hard stop when it is the core of the job.

## Not for me
Functions you never want, whatever the title says (for example: pre-sales, QA).

## Title traps
Titles that match your words but mean something else at some companies.
