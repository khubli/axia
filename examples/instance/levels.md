# My level, and what it is called elsewhere

Axia reads this with your resume to label each role a **step up**, the **same level** or a **step down**
from where you are now. Titles mislead across employers (one bank's Vice President is another's Director),
so say what your level is called at the companies you watch. Editing this file re-labels the roles on
your dashboard over the next run or two; it never changes their scores.

## Where I am now
Staff Engineer at Example Bank, leading a group of 3 teams (about 20 engineers). 14 years' experience.

## The same level as me
| Company | One step down | Same level | One step up |
|---|---|---|---|
| Example Bank | Senior Engineer, AVP | Staff Engineer, VP | Principal Engineer, Director |
| Contoso Pay | Engineering Manager | Senior Engineering Manager | Director of Engineering |
| Big Tech Co | L5 | L6 | L7 |

## When the title doesn't settle it
- 8 to 12 years asked, or leading one team: a step down.
- 12 to 18 years, or leading several teams: the same level.
- Leading managers, an organisation of 50 or more, or a regional remit: a step up.
